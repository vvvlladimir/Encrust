# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What the situation is. The forces in play: constraints, requirements, what the project
already committed to, what would break. Facts, not opinions. Enough that a reader who was
not in the conversation understands why a choice had to be made at all.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and in the present tense. "We use X." Include the
specific version, boundary or convention where that is the substance of the decision.

## Consequences

What follows from this, good and bad. What becomes easy, what becomes hard, what now has
to be maintained, what has to be revisited if an assumption changes. Name the signal that
would make us reopen this.

## Alternatives considered

### Option that was rejected

Why it was on the table, and the specific reason it lost.

### The option that won, and what it costs

The honest downside of the decision above. An ADR without this section filled in is not
finished.
