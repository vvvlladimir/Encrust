# Language rules

Read `docs/design/i18n.md` before adding, changing or removing a string, and
`docs/design/i18n/en-style.md` before writing English.

- Every string is `tr!("key", ...)`, English in `assets/i18n/en.ftl` under the group of the
  file that shows it; one sentence a message, its objects as arguments, never assembled.
- English is the source: one term a concept, no idioms, a `#` comment where its use is not
  in its words; a new term goes into every `docs/design/i18n/<code>-glossary.md`.
- A new meaning is a new key, the old one deleted from every catalogue; only a rewording
  with the same meaning and arguments keeps its key.
- Nothing is sized to English: a new or reworded label is looked at in Russian, and what
  does not fit is shortened in its catalogue or cut by the widget, never by a wider layout.
- A translation edit follows that language's `<code>-style.md` and `<code>-glossary.md`;
  a new language follows "Adding a language" in `i18n.md`, its style and glossary first.
- Done: the `i18n-editor` agent has passed every changed message;
  `cargo test -p encrust-app --lib i18n`; `cargo xtask i18n <code>` lists only what waits.
